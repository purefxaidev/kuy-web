Privacy Policy (Draft)

Draft — not yet reviewed by a lawyer. Effective date to be set at launch.

  • Recordings, transcripts and chat history are stored locally on your device by default.
  • Nothing is uploaded unless you enable cloud backup, cloud AI, or cloud voice and confirm consent.
  • When cloud voice (TTS) is enabled, only the reply text is sent to our server to generate audio; your voice is not sent.
  • You can delete local data in the app and delete your account and all server data (PDPA right to erasure).
  • Friends & chat: messages and files you send to friends are stored on our server, encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. They are not end-to-end encrypted yet.
  • Voice/video calls connect device-to-device and are encrypted with DTLS-SRTP; when a direct path is unavailable, encrypted media may pass through a relay (TURN) server. Calls are not recorded; only call metadata (who, when, duration) is logged.
  • Friend invites are single-use QR codes that expire after 10 minutes. You can report or block users.
  • Deleting your account erases your account and all server data, including messages, files, friendships and call history.
  • Planned features (feed, reels, community relay/peer-to-peer sharing) are in development and are not active. This policy will be updated before they launch; peer sharing will be opt-in.
  • Microphone access is used only while you talk or record.

Data controller contact: to be provided by PureFxAI before launch.
